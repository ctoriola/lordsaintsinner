STORY
Our story

I am all three: a lord of the craft, a saint on the best days and a sinner the rest of the time. The songs keep the whole person.

Lord, Saint, Sinner began in 2016 with a borrowed mixing desk in Yaba and a first four-track EP recorded over one restless weekend.

The project grew by following the song: late sessions, cheap rooms, long walks home and the feeling that a rough idea might become a world.

Today it is still one voice, one catalogue and one ongoing conversation between the sacred and the reckless. Lagos and London are the coordinates; the music is the address.

The name & the mark

Crown, halo,
flame

Our logo carries all three sides of the name: the crown for the lord, the halo for the saint and the grinning flame for the sinner. The badge signs off everything we release; the wordmark is how we announce ourselves.

What we believe

Our values

Four principles written on the studio wall on day one. We’ve repainted twice; the words stayed.

01

Artist first

Every decision starts with one question: is this good for the artist’s long-term career?

02

Fair by design

Transparent royalty statements, plain-language contracts and master ownership that reverts.

03

Local roots, global reach

We build from scenes we know intimately, then take them to every stage in the world.

04

Play loud

We back originality over trends. If it sounds like everything else, it isn’t for us.

Timeline

A decade
of noise

From a borrowed mixing desk to a festival for 40,000 people. The milestones that shaped us.

  1. 2016

    The first spark

    Lord, Saint, Sinner is founded in Yaba, Lagos. First release: the Kindling EP.

  2. 2018

    First Gold record

    KAIRO’s breakout single goes Gold in Nigeria and Ghana, funding our first full-time team.

  3. 2020

    The words deepen

    The writing turns inward, making room for the harder songs and the stranger truths.

  4. 2022

    London calling

    A second office and studio open in Hackney, alongside our First Light compilation.

  5. 2024

    Heatwave Festival

    Our own two-day festival launches in Lagos and sells out 40,000 tickets in its first year.

  6. 2026

    Ten years, still independent

    Starfall becomes our biggest release ever. We open Studio Ember, our Dolby Atmos suite.
