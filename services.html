How it works

From spark
to song

No gatekeeping, no mystery. Here’s exactly what happens after you send us your music.

  1. Send your demo

    Share up to three tracks through our contact form. Private links are fine — no attachments needed.

  2. We listen. All of it.

    Our A&R team reviews every submission within four weeks. If it moves us, we’ll call.

  3. Get to know each other

    Studio visit, a session or two, and an honest conversation about your goals and what you need.

  4. Let the song lead

    Follow the lyric, protect the feeling, and leave enough room for the record to become more honest than the first idea.

  5. Light it up

    A release plan, a team, and a campaign built around you. Then we go again.

FAQ

Good
questions

Still curious? Ask us directly

Do I keep ownership of my masters?

On licence and distribution deals, yes — always. On full label deals, masters revert to you after an agreed term, typically 7–10 years. It’s in writing from day one.

Do you only work with signed artists?

No. Studio, mixing, mastering, sync and live production are available to independent artists and other labels too.

What does it sound like?

It moves between R&B, hip-hop, electronic textures and whatever the song needs. The only rule is that it has to feel true.

How long does a demo review take?

Up to four weeks. We reply to every submission, even if it’s a no for now.

How do the songs take shape?

They begin with a line, a rhythm or a feeling that will not leave. The rest is patience, noise and knowing when to stop.

Where are your studios?

Yaba, Lagos and Hackney, London. Both are bookable by the day or week — get in touch for rates.
